This subset uses 24 public high-quality MP3 previews: the first four recorded strengths
of the six open strings of a Yamaha Eterna classical guitar, finger-plucked and
recorded with a CAD E100 microphone. Every selected sound's license was checked
on its individual Freesound page. Per-file source URLs and hashes are listed in
scripts/guitar-sources/quartertone.json; docs/research/quartertone-audition.md
provides a linked inventory.

Changes: convert to mono 44.1 kHz MP3, fade the final 300 ms of each file,
omit the strongest fifth tier, transpose at playback to the configured chord,
and apply one level trim to the complete library. No looping or reverb.
Original WAVs and verified sampler loop headers are
not included; these are finite, compressed preview recordings.
