Guitar recording: Raimundo 118, performed by Brian Penkrot, December 11, 2011.
Recorded by Shane Hoose and Zach Zubow in the anechoic chamber with an Earthworks
QTC40 microphone, at a distance of five feet.
Instrument/source page: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISguitar.html
Project: Lawrence Fritts, University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.

Audition changes: isolate the initial/open note from each pp/mf/ff chromatic scale;
remove subsonic rumble with a 30 Hz high-pass filter; fade the last 150 ms; convert
to mono 44.1 kHz MP3. No pitch shifting, looping, reverb or per-note normalization.
One level trim is applied to the complete library. Exact URLs, source hashes,
and note end times are retained in manifest.json and the preparation script.
